Education Hub

📚 Learn & Resources

A step-by-step learning flow that matches your reference style and also carries more useful stock trading information.

4 STEPS

01

Understand Trading

Trading means buying a stock and selling it later based on a planned opportunity, not random guessing.

  • Short-term or long-term approach
  • Entry, target, and exit matter
  • Always define your risk first
02

Meet Requirements

Before you buy stocks, set up the essentials properly so you can trade smoothly and safely.

  • Trading account and verification
  • Linked bank funds
  • Budget and capital discipline
03

Build Knowledge

Strong decisions come from learning how the market, business data, and chart behavior actually work.

  • Fundamental analysis basics
  • Technical analysis and candles
  • Support, resistance, and volume
04

Take Action Smartly

Choose a stock, place a planned trade, review the result, and keep improving your process with discipline.

  • Start small as a beginner
  • Use stop-loss and position sizing
  • Avoid emotional decisions
Stock trading becomes easier to understand when you learn in steps.

Start by understanding what trading is, prepare your account and capital, build the right knowledge, and then take small planned actions. This section is designed to give beginners a clearer path before they start buying or selling stocks.

Trading Awareness

Trading can create profit, but it also carries risk. Prices move based on news, market sentiment, business performance, and overall economic conditions.

Before placing any order, users should understand the company, check market trends, decide a stop-loss, and avoid trading only on emotion or tips from others.

Beginners should start with small amounts, learn risk management, and remember that protecting capital is as important as earning returns.
